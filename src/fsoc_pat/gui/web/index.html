📡 Optical SNR & Radiometry Analysis

EXCELLENT
SNR_{dB} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left( \frac{P_{rx}^2}{\sigma_{shot}^2 + \sigma_{thermal}^2} \right)

Electrical signal-to-noise ratio at the photodetector transimpedance amplifier (TIA) stage. Evaluates optical signal power relative to noise equivalent power (NEP = 2.5 pW/√Hz) across the 1.25 Gbps bandwidth. Maintains a wide fade reserve above the 15.6 dB threshold required for error-free telecom operation.

⚡ Bit-Error Rate & Telecom Integrity

OPTIMAL
BER = \frac{1}{2} \text{erfc}\left( \frac{Q}{\sqrt{2}} \right) = \frac{1}{2} \text{erfc}\left( \frac{\sqrt{SNR}}{2\sqrt{2}} \right)

Probability of bit error for On-Off Keying (OOK) direct detection. The system operates substantially below the hard-decision forward error correction (FEC) limit of 1.0e-6, ensuring error-free telecommunication frames without packet dropouts even during sudden platform maneuvers.
